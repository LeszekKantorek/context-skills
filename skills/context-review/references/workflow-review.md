# Reviewing the complete workflow

Use this scope when asked to assess the skills or the route from source to future action. Bound the review to accessible skill versions, task records, and project evidence. Current instructions do not establish what an older session actually saw.

Trace a consequential episode through these responsibilities:

| Responsibility | Useful question |
| --- | --- |
| Gather | Was the relevant source found, and was a missing decision distinguished from missing information? |
| Apply | What action reflected the source, and did the result preserve its constraint? |
| Consolidate | Was a durable change recorded with its evidence and uncertainty at a useful location? |
| Review | Could later work retrieve current knowledge, and did the repair address the observed problem? |

Look for duplicated searches or reports, missing handoffs, accidental mandatory sequencing, and excessive structure. A known entry should not require a full gather pass. No durable learning should not produce a filler note. Review should not rewrite the collection merely to fit a layout.

When CDE is installed, examine whether discover, maintain, and audit reuse the context operation's result. Investigations, authority decisions, implementation validation, effects, and task handoff retain their distinct purposes. Do not assign credit to every skill for the same outcome or infer a skill's contribution from a name mention.

For each proposed workflow repair, give the failing episode, the smallest change, and a contrasting case that should keep working. Improvements may belong in source knowledge, tools, or instructions; they do not always justify another skill. Change skill definitions only within the requested authoring scope.
